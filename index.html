To You, From Me

You're the first person I want to tell everything to — the good stuff, the dumb stuff, the 2 a.m. thoughts I probably shouldn't share.

You have this way of making ordinary days feel like something worth remembering. A random text from you, a voice note, the way you say my name — it all stays with me longer than it should.

I don't know exactly what love is supposed to feel like at 15. But if it feels like this — light and warm and a little bit terrifying — then I think I'm doing it right.

— Tobi

A Few Things I Promise

I'm 15, so I can't promise I'll get everything right. But I can promise this:

  • I'll listen — actually listen, not just wait for my turn to talk.
  • I'll keep trying to make you laugh, even when my jokes are terrible.
  • I'll be proud of you out loud — not just in my head or my notes app.
  • I'll choose you. On the easy days and the ones that aren't.
  • And I'll keep showing up. Every single day. Even the boring ones.
